Movie Review: Backrooms

I just watched Kane Parsons' Backrooms movie and I genuinely have no words. Seeing something I've followed since 2022 go from a tiny community of 100-1,000 people to a massive movie is surreal. The cinematography, characters, worldbuilding, attention to detail, sound design, and music were incredible.

But the story is what completely changed my view of the series. What I once thought was "liminal horror + monsters + government and corporate politics" has evolved into something infinitely more complex (no pun intended).Ivan Beck and his background, A-Sync, the concept of Still-Lifes, Null Zones, the expanding Complex, the DOE, the Sun/Sea motifs, SO much more going on than I ever imagined. Kane has created a world that feels scientific, mysterious, interconnected, and genuinely alive instead of relying on "scary monster" horror.

Watching this evolution has been absolutely insane, and I genuinely believe Kane Parsons is going to be one of the greatest directors of my generation (Gen Z).

This isn't just a masterpiece. It feels like the beginning of something that could change filmmaking forever!

Movie Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (10/10)
