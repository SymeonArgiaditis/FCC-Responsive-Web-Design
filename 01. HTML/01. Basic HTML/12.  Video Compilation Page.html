Networking/Cybersecurity

Networking/Cybersecurity involves designing, securing, and maintaining the systems that connect devices and protect data across them. It focuses on ensuring reliable communication, safeguarding information from threats, and maintaining the integrity of digital infrastructure. Professionals in this field blend technical troubleshooting with a defensive mindset to keep systems running and secure.

Networking

Networking forms the backbone of digital communication by defining how devices connect, exchange data, and route information across local and global systems. It uses protocols, addressing schemes, and hardware like routers and switches to enable devices to communicate reliably.

Security Protocols

Security Protocols are used to protect data as it travels across networks, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and authentication between communicating systems. They include encryption standards, secure handshakes, and access controls, making sure sensitive information stays protected. Well-implemented protocols also support compliance with security standards across industries.

Threat Detection

Threat Detection is the practice of identifying and responding to malicious activity, enabling systems to flag intrusions, block attacks, and adapt defenses based on emerging vulnerabilities and attacker behavior.
